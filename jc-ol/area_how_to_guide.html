Area of a Rectangle

Questions 1 – 3 on your worksheet

Formula Area = Length × Width
📋 Step-by-Step
  • 1 Write down the length and width. Make sure they are in the same units (both in cm, both in m, etc.).
  • 2 Multiply them together: Area = Length × Width
  • 3 Write your answer with the correct squared unit, e.g. 30cm²
✅ Worked Example – Q1
Length = 5cm, Width = 6cm
→5 × 6 = 30
Answer: 30 cm²
✅ Worked Example – Q3
35mm must become 3.5cm
→3.5 × 3 = 10.5
Answer: 10.5 cm²
⚠️ Watch Out!
  • If one side is in mm and the other in cm, convert first! (10mm = 1cm)
  • If one side is in m and the other in cm, convert first! (1m = 100cm)
  • Always include cm² (or m², etc.) in your answer — never forget the units!

Missing Side of a Rectangle

Questions 4 – 6 on your worksheet

You already know the Area and one side. To find the missing side, divide.

Rearranging the Formula
Area = Length × Width
→ Missing side = Area ÷ Known side
📋 Step-by-Step
  • 1 Check that all measurements are in the same units. Convert if needed.
  • 2 Divide the Area by the side you know: Missing side = Area ÷ Known side
  • 3 Write the answer with the correct unit (cm, m, etc.) — not cm²!
✅ Worked Example – Q4
Area = 28cm², Length = 4cm
→28 ÷ 4 = 7
Width = 7 cm
✅ Worked Example – Q6
Length = 0.092m → 9.2cm
→32.2 ÷ 9.2 = 3.5
Width = 3.5 cm

Area of a Triangle

Questions 1 – 3 on your worksheet

Formula Area = ½ × Base × Height

You can also write this as: Area = 0.5 × Base × Height  (same thing!)

💡 What is the Vertical Height?
  • It is the straight-up height of the triangle — the perpendicular distance from the base to the top. It is not the slanted side!
📋 Step-by-Step
  • 1 Make sure the base and height are in the same units. Convert if needed.
  • 2 Multiply: 0.5 × Base × Height
  • 3 Write your answer with cm² (or the correct squared unit).
✅ Worked Example – Q1
Base = 3cm, Height = 7cm
→0.5 × 3 × 7 = 10.5
Answer: 10.5 cm²
✅ Worked Example – Q3
42mm = 4.2cm, Height = 5cm
→0.5 × 4.2 × 5 = 10.5
Answer: 10.5 cm²
⚠️ Watch Out!
  • The hypotenuse (the longest slanted side of a right-angled triangle) is NOT the height.
  • Remember to multiply by 0.5 (or ½) — students often forget this!
  • Convert mm → cm by dividing by 10 before you start.
🔍

Missing Side of a Triangle

Questions 4 – 6 on your worksheet

You know the Area and one side. Rearrange the formula to find the missing side.

Rearranging the Formula
Area = 0.5 × Base × Height
→ Height = Area ÷ (0.5 × Base)
→ Base   = Area ÷ (0.5 × Height)
📋 Step-by-Step
  • 1 Convert everything to the same units first.
  • 2 Multiply the known side by 0.5: 0.5 × known side
  • 3 Divide the Area by your answer from Step 2.
✅ Worked Example – Q4
Area = 30cm², Base = 6cm
→0.5 × 6 = 3
→30 ÷ 3 = 10
Height = 10 cm
✅ Worked Example – Q6
0.055m → 5.5cm
→0.5 × 5.5 = 2.75
→12.2 ÷ 2.75 ≈ 4.44
Height ≈ 4.44 cm

Units – The Golden Rule

Always sort your units FIRST before calculating

📐
10mm = 1cm
📐
100cm = 1m
📐
1000m = 1km

💡 Convert to the unit asked for in the question before you multiply. If the question says "give your answer in cm²", make sure both measurements are already in cm.

⚡ Quick Reference – All Formulas

🟦 Rectangle Area
A = L × W
🟦 Missing Rectangle Side
Side = A ÷ other side
🔺 Triangle Area
A = 0.5 × b × h
🔺 Missing Triangle Side
Side = A ÷ (0.5 × other)
